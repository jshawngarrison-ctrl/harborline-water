FAQ

Straight answers

Is Charleston municipal water unsafe?

No. Charleston Water System and Mount Pleasant Waterworks meet EPA standards. We don’t market fear. We help with taste, odor, chloramine residual, ice/coffee quality, and how water feels on skin and hair.

Why catalytic carbon instead of a softener?

Charleston Water System reports average hardness around 58.4 ppm / 3.4 gpg — moderately soft. Softeners shine on hard well water. For most city homes here, catalytic carbon targeting chloramine and taste/odor is the better first move.

What’s included in the $2,700–$3,100 price?

A typical whole-home catalytic carbon system installed by a licensed plumber for a standard city home. Exact equipment and any add-ons (like under-sink RO) are confirmed after your free test and a look at your plumbing.

Do you install reverse osmosis?

Yes — as an optional under-sink system for drinking and cooking water. Many homeowners pair whole-home carbon with RO at the kitchen sink. Learn more about RO.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-home installs are completed in a few hours by a licensed plumber partner. We’ll schedule after you approve the recommendation — typically within about a week depending on partner capacity.

What about PFAS or “contaminated water” claims?

We stick to utility-aligned facts and certified reduction data for the media we install. We won’t use scare language about municipal water being unsafe. If you have a specific lab concern, bring it to the free test and we’ll talk through options honestly.
