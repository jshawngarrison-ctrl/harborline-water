The local water story

Johns Island is a split market. Many homes receive purchased Charleston Water System–treated water (via St. John’s Water Company and wholesale arrangements) — so the chloramine taste story matches the peninsula. For those homes, our city catalytic carbon package is the right fit.

Rural pockets — especially along River Road and similar corridors — often sit on private wells with iron, sediment, or sulfur (“rotten egg”) issues. That is a separate well package, not the same SKU as city carbon.

Tell us city vs well on the form. We will not sell you a municipal carbon system for a well problem — or a well iron system for a chloramine taste issue.

What we recommend most often

  • Whole-home catalytic carbon — hero offer for municipal homes
  • Optional under-sink RO — drinking & cooking polish
  • Softener — only when tested hardness justifies it
  • Well package — separate path for private wells
