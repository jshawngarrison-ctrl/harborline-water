Why wells get their own path

Municipal homes on Charleston Water System or Mount Pleasant Waterworks share a chloramine / taste story. Private wells are unregulated at the tap and frequently show iron staining, hydrogen sulfide (“rotten egg” smell), sediment, hardness, or bacteria concerns that need UV or disinfection follow-up.

Those problems need different media, tanks, and sometimes multi-stage trains. Mixing them into a city carbon quote creates bad outcomes — so Harborline keeps well work as a separate package with its own testing and pricing.

Common well concerns we evaluate

  • Iron / rust staining on fixtures and laundry
  • Sulfur / H2S odor
  • Sediment and turbidity
  • Hardness (softeners often belong here)
  • Bacteria / UV needs (when indicated by testing)
