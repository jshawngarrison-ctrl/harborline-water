When RO makes sense

Whole-home catalytic carbon improves water at every tap. Under-sink RO goes further for water you drink and cook with — reducing dissolved solids and polishing taste at a dedicated faucet (and often the refrigerator line).

RO is an add-on, not a requirement. Many Harborline customers start with whole-home carbon alone; others want both.

What to expect

  • Compact system under the kitchen sink
  • Dedicated drinking faucet (style options available)
  • Optional fridge ice-maker connection
  • Periodic filter / membrane changes (we explain the schedule)

Pricing: RO is quoted as an add-on after your free water test — typically hundreds to low thousands depending on configuration. Your whole-home city package remains in the $2,700–$3,100 band.

Prefer whole-house first? Start with catalytic carbon.

✦ Honest pairing advice

If your main complaint is shower odor or whole-house taste, start with point-of-entry carbon. If you mainly care about drinking water at one sink, RO alone can be enough — we will say which fits.
