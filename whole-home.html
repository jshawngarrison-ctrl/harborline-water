Built for Lowcountry city water

Charleston Water System and Mount Pleasant Waterworks use chloramines for disinfection. Standard carbon can struggle with chloramine; catalytic carbon is better suited to reduce that residual so water tastes and smells cleaner at every fixture.

This is not a claim that municipal water is unsafe. Utilities meet EPA standards. Harborline sells comfort: better-tasting ice and coffee, less chemical odor in the shower, and a softer feel on skin and hair.

What a typical install includes

  • Whole-home (point-of-entry) catalytic carbon system sized for a typical city home
  • Licensed plumber installation at the main line
  • Post-install check so you can taste the difference
  • Clear guidance on media life and maintenance

Price band: Most city homes land at $2,700–$3,100 installed. Final quote after your free water test and a look at your plumbing layout.

Often paired with

Optional under-sink reverse osmosis for drinking and cooking. Softener only if hardness testing supports it — CWS averages about 3.4 gpg.
