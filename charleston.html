The local water story

Much of the peninsula and surrounding areas are served by Charleston Water System, which draws from the Bushy Park Reservoir and Edisto River and treats water at the Hanahan plant. Official treatment uses chlorine dioxide and chloramines (chlorine + ammonia) for disinfection.

That disinfection keeps municipal water meeting EPA standards — and it can leave a noticeable taste or odor at the tap, especially in ice and coffee. Surface-water algae can also contribute earthy or musty notes seasonally. Harborline focuses on whole-home catalytic carbon to improve what you taste and feel, without claiming your water is unsafe.

Hardness note: CWS FAQ states average hardness around 58.4 ppm / 3.4 gpg — moderately soft. A softener is optional for most Charleston city homes, not a default.
