The local water story

Mount Pleasant is served by Mount Pleasant Waterworks — a separate utility from Charleston Water System, also using chloramines for disinfection. Hardness is generally low to moderate depending on the source blend; it is not “desert hard.”

East Cooper homeowners often notice chloramine taste in coffee, ice makers, and showers first. Our recommendation for most MPW homes is the same mid-market wedge: whole-home catalytic carbon sized for the house, with optional reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink.

Honest softener note: We test hardness on-site. If your water is already in a soft-to-moderate range, we will say so and will not push a salt softener you do not need.

What we recommend most often

  • Whole-home catalytic carbon — hero offer for municipal homes
  • Optional under-sink RO — drinking & cooking polish
  • Softener — only when tested hardness justifies it
  • Well package — separate path for private wells
